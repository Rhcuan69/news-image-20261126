26 November 2026, drawn by a pure-stdlib Python program
(`main.py`, no third-party packages, no network).

## The day's stories

- **Gulf oil crisis (hero):** Yemen's Houthis say they struck Ras
  Tanura, Saudi Arabia's largest refinery, and satellite images show
  thick black smoke; Saudi defences downed four drones. Two tankers
  hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire. Brent crude
  jumped past $93 a barrel.
- **United States:** Thanksgiving Day, with the 100th Macy's parade
  in New York and about 81.5 million Americans travelling.
- **Space:** China's Shenzhou-24 docks with Tiangong, carrying
  Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first Pakistani in orbit and
  the first foreign astronaut aboard the station.
- **Bangladesh:** the garment minimum wage rises 36% to 17,000 taka
  after weeks of protests that shut factories.
- **Gaza:** an Israeli airstrike kills Hamas security chief Mohammad
  Abuelouf, his wife and two children; the year-old ceasefire is
  under strain.
- **India:** 18 years since the 26/11 Mumbai attacks; Constitution
  Day.
- **Also today:** the Australia–Solomon Islands security treaty is
  signed; the US readies a new tariff plan; Vaibhav Sooryavanshi,
  15, hits 204 off 53 balls.

All stories were first reported on 26 November 2026, or carried as
that day's news in its morning papers. Sources include Reuters, AP,
the White House, the Australian Prime Minister's office, Nature and
AAA.

## Run

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

Writes `entry.png` (1024x1024, deterministic: two runs produce
byte-identical output).
